# Best AI Clip Generators for Turning Long Videos into Shorts in 2026

The best AI clip generator is not always the one that creates the most clips.

A tool can generate 20 clips from a podcast and still miss the one moment people would actually share. Another tool can create only five clips but give you cleaner captions, better framing, and titles that are easier to publish.

For this guide, we are looking at one job:

> Take a long video and turn it into short videos that are good enough to review, edit, and publish.

That means we care about clip selection, captions, aspect ratios, export limits, watermarks, and how much cleanup is needed before posting.

## How to judge an AI clip generator

Do not judge these tools only by how polished the homepage looks. Use a real source video and check five things:

1. Did it find a clip you would actually post?
2. Did the clip make sense without the full video?
3. Were the subtitles usable without heavy cleanup?
4. Could you adjust framing and aspect ratio easily?
5. Could you export cleanly without hitting a watermark or credit limit?

A 60-second clip with a clean hook and a clear ending is better than ten random cuts.

## 2. OpusClip — best one-click AI clipping tool for most creators

OpusClip is the tool most people think of first when they search for AI clipping. Its homepage describes the product as a way to turn a long video into short clips and publish them to social platforms in one click.  
Source: [OpusClip homepage](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)

Its free plan currently includes 60 credits per month, up to 1080p rendered clips, auto reframe, AI captions, and a watermark. It also says clips stop being exportable after 3 days on the free plan.  
Source: [OpusClip pricing](https://www.opus.pro/pricing)

### What works well

OpusClip is fast. It is also familiar, which matters if you are sending recommendations to a team or creator who has already heard of it.

It works especially well for:

- podcasts
- interviews
- creator talking-head videos
- educational videos
- business videos
- videos with strong quotable moments

### Where it may fall short

The free plan is not a clean publishing workflow because of the watermark. Also, AI highlight detection can still miss the clip a human editor would choose, especially in videos where context matters more than energy.

## 3. Vizard — best for repurposing videos with a simple workflow

Vizard gives creators a practical long-video-to-short-video workflow. The free plan currently includes 60 credits per month, AI-generated clips, 720p export, full editor access, and 3-day storage. The paid Creator plan is positioned around no watermark, 4K export, social scheduling, and videos stored forever.  
Source: [Vizard pricing](https://vizard.ai/pricing)

### What works well

Vizard feels clear. Upload, generate clips, edit, export. That is enough for many creators.

It is useful for:

- marketing videos
- webinars
- podcast clips
- internal training videos
- social media snippets

### Where it may fall short

If your workflow depends on deeper storytelling, Vizard may still feel like a general-purpose clip generator. It is good at repurposing, not necessarily at rebuilding a narrative.

## 4. Klap — best for simple podcast and interview clips

Klap is a focused AI clip generator. The current yearly-billed entry plan is listed at $23/month and includes 10 video uploads monthly, videos up to 45 minutes, 100 generated clips, and HD download.  
Source: [Klap pricing](https://klap.app/pricing)

### What works well

Klap keeps the workflow simple. It is a good option when your source videos are mostly podcasts, interviews, talking-head YouTube videos, or straightforward creator videos.

If your video has clear speakers and quote-worthy moments, Klap is worth testing.

### Where it may fall short

The entry plan has upload and video-length limits. If you process long webinars, multi-hour streams, or a lot of raw footage each month, check the plan limits before committing.

## 5. Descript — best for transcript-based editing and clips

Descript is a better fit if you want to edit the video itself, not only generate clips from it.

The free plan includes 60 media minutes per month and 100 one-time AI credits. The Hobbyist plan includes 10 media hours, 400 AI credits, 1080p watermark-free export, and AI tools. The Creator plan includes 30 media hours, 800 AI credits, and 4K watermark-free export.  
Source: [Descript pricing](https://www.descript.com/pricing)

### What works well

Descript is strong when you care about the script, transcript, voice, and full episode workflow. It is especially good for creators who record podcasts, interviews, tutorials, or courses.

### Where it may fall short

It is not the fastest tool if you only want a batch of social clips. Descript gives you more control, but that also means you may spend more time editing.

## 6. CapCut — best free editor after the AI finds a clip

CapCut is not always the strongest AI clip generator. But it is one of the most useful tools for finishing clips.

CapCut’s homepage describes its online editor as free, with AI tools, subtitles, and HD export without watermark. Its Standard vs Pro page says the free version covers basic editing, filters, transitions, audio enhancements, text, stickers, and quick social content, while some premium assets and advanced AI tools require Pro.  
Source: [CapCut homepage](https://www.capcut.com/), [CapCut Standard vs Pro](https://www.capcut.com/resource/capcut-standard-vs-pro)

### What works well

CapCut is excellent when a creator wants a hands-on edit: captions, pacing, sound, stickers, transitions, and social formatting.

### Where it may fall short

If you need the tool to choose the best moments from a long video automatically, CapCut is not always the first place to start. Use it as a finishing tool.

## 7. VEED — best for quick browser edits with subtitles

VEED is a browser-based video editor that covers editing, subtitles, resizing, and AI video features. The free online editor page says free users can use basic editing tools and export in 720p, but free exports include a watermark.  
Source: [VEED video editor](https://www.veed.io/tools/video-editor)

### What works well

VEED is convenient when you need a quick browser workflow and do not want to install desktop editing software.

### Where it may fall short

The watermark means the free plan is mainly for testing. For public-facing brand videos, you will probably need a paid plan.

## 8. Kapwing — best for team-based clipping and resizing

Kapwing is useful for teams that produce many short videos in different formats.

Its free plan includes 10 credits, unlimited exports with a watermark, export up to 1 minute, and 720p video quality. The Pro plan removes the watermark, supports 4K exports, uploads up to 6GB, exports projects up to 2 hours, and includes up to 1,000 minutes per month of auto-subtitling.  
Source: [Kapwing pricing](https://www.kapwing.com/pricing)

### What works well

Kapwing is strong for collaboration, resizing, subtitles, brand assets, and browser editing.

### Where it may fall short

The free plan is limited. If you want to publish serious clips every week, treat Kapwing as a paid tool.

## FAQ

### What is an AI clip generator?

An AI clip generator takes a long video and identifies shorter sections that could work as social videos. Better tools also add captions, reframe for vertical video, generate titles, and help export to Shorts, TikTok, or Reels.

### What is the best AI clip generator for podcasts?

Klap, OpusClip, Vizard, and Descript are good starting points. Use Klap or OpusClip for speed, Descript for transcript editing, and Vizard for a simple repurposing workflow.

### What is the best AI clip generator for movie recaps?

Use a tool that handles story structure. NarratoAI fits here because movie recap clips often need context, not just a single highlight moment.

### Can AI clip generators replace an editor?

Not fully. They can reduce the first-pass work, but most clips still need a human review for accuracy, timing, captions, and context.
