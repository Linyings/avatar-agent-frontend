# NarratoAI Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 9, 2025**
**Effective date: November 9, 2025**
**Version: v1.0**

## Introduction

Welcome to NarratoAI. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices and rights available to you.

Please read this policy carefully before using NarratoAI. By using or continuing to use our services, you agree that we may collect, use, store, and process your information as described here.

## Information We Collect

### Account information

When you create an account, we may collect your email address, username, encrypted password, and optional OAuth profile information such as a third-party user ID, display name, and avatar.

### Service usage information

When you use NarratoAI, we may process uploaded video files, generated subtitles, analysis results, editing records, project metadata, operation logs, device information, browser information, IP address, and similar data needed to provide and secure the service.

### Payment information

If you purchase paid services, we may process transaction identifiers, transaction amounts, payment status, order details, and billing-related records. Payment processing is handled by third-party payment or billing providers such as WeChat Pay or Creem. We do not directly collect or store sensitive card numbers or payment passwords.

### Sensitive information in content

Videos you upload may contain faces, voices, or other sensitive information. You are responsible for ensuring that you have lawful rights and permissions for uploaded content. We process such content only to provide the requested service and apply reasonable security controls.

## How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- create and manage your account;
- authenticate users and protect account security;
- provide video upload, storage, analysis, subtitle, voiceover, editing, and export features;
- process purchases, subscriptions, refunds, taxes, and receipts;
- maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the service;
- send service notices, security alerts, and support communications;
- comply with legal, regulatory, accounting, and dispute-resolution obligations.

## Storage and Retention

We retain account information while your account exists and for a limited period after closure when required for security, dispute handling, or legal compliance. Uploaded videos, project data, generated outputs, logs, and payment records are retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

## Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary information with service providers that help us operate NarratoAI, such as cloud infrastructure, AI model providers, analytics tools, and payment or billing providers. These providers are expected to process information only for the services they provide to us.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, public authorities, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of NarratoAI, our users, or the public.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted transmission, access controls, authentication controls, operational logging, backup processes, and internal security practices. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we work to reduce risk and respond to security issues responsibly.

## Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, copy, correct, delete, or withdraw consent for certain personal information. You can manage some account information directly in the product, or contact us for assistance.

## Minors

NarratoAI is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child without proper authorization, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, improve reliability, and understand service usage. You can manage browser storage through your browser settings, but disabling some storage may affect product functionality.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are material, we will notify you through the product, email, or other reasonable means. The updated policy takes effect on the date shown above unless otherwise stated.
